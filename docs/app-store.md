## Description (4000)
Did you do enough today? Do-Du answers with data, not guilt.

Give each project a daily target, start the timer when you begin, and Do-Du tells you how much is left. When every project scheduled for today hits its target, the day is enough, and stopping is allowed.

TIMER FIRST
• Start a session in one tap, with or without a task. Quick sessions can stay unassigned and be moved to a project later.
• One timer at a time: starting a new one stops the last at the same instant, so time is never counted twice.
• See today's sessions on a timeline, switch tasks or projects without stopping, and get a nudge if a timer runs too long.

A CLEAR DAILY VERDICT
• Not yet: exactly how much is left today.
• Enough: every scheduled project has met its target.
• Rest day: nothing is scheduled, and that's normal.
• Choose which days each project counts, and when your day starts, so late-night work belongs to the day before.

TASKS AND PROJECTS
• Tasks grouped by project, with categories, due dates and estimates.
• Quick Add: type "Invoice Client X tomorrow ~30m #admin" and Do-Du fills in the project, due date, estimate and category. Parsing runs on your device.
• Import tasks from Apple Reminders or a Notion database.

STATS
• Day, week and month totals by project and category.
• How many days were enough, plus a daily trend.
• Export every session as a CSV file.

ASSISTANT (OPTIONAL)
• Plan My Day suggests which tasks to work on and for how long, using your open tasks, today's targets and your calendar.
• Recap writes a short summary of your day or week.
• Chat answers questions such as "How much deep work did I do last week?"
• Uses Claude with your own Anthropic API key, stored in your Keychain. Nothing is saved until you accept it, and every number comes from your own data.

ON IPHONE
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and a Live Activity with the running timer in the Dynamic Island.
• Start and stop from Siri and Shortcuts.

ON MAC
• A full window with sidebar, a menu-bar timer, and Quick Add from any app with Option-Command-K.
• Command-Shift-T starts or stops the timer from anywhere.
• The timer stops automatically when your Mac sleeps or sits idle, backdated to your last activity.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your data syncs between your devices through your own iCloud account, or stays on one device if you turn sync off.
• No account, no ads, no tracking.

Do-Du is for one person who wants to know when the day's work is done.

## App Privacy (Connect questionnaire)
- **Data collected by the developer:** none. No analytics, no accounts, no tracking.
- **iCloud:** data syncs through the user's private CloudKit database (not developer-accessible).
- **Assistant:** only when the user adds an API key and uses Plan, Recap or Chat, the needed tasks and totals are sent to Anthropic under the user's own key. Disclose in the privacy policy; it is not developer data collection.

## Review notes

- Calendar and Reminders access are optional and only requested from Settings or Import.
